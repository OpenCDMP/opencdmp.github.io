# Language & region

The **Language & region** page sets what new users of the organisation start with. Each user can still change these in their own [profile](/docs/using/profile-settings.md#language--region).

## Authorization

Open to the **System Administrator** and **Organisation Admin** roles. See [Roles](/docs/administering/how-it-fits-together.md#roles).

## Navigation

Switch the sidebar to the **Admin** view, choose **Settings** under **Organisation**, then **Language & region** in the **Appearance** group. See [Organisation settings](/docs/administering/tenant-management/tenant-configuration.md).

## Defaults for new users

- **Language**: the language of the application. It also comes first in the language menu and is shown to visitors who are not signed in.
- **Timezone**: used to show dates and times.
- **Format**: how dates and numbers are written, with an example of the result.

The languages people can pick, and your own wording for any text, are managed on [Languages & texts](/docs/administering/system-configuration/languages.md).

# Your first plan

This tutorial takes you from logging in to a finished Data Management Plan (DMP) or Software Management Plan (SMP): you pick a blueprint, answer its questions, describe your research outputs as assets, invite your team, finalize the plan, and download or publish it. Each step links to the page that covers it in full.

Plan on 15 to 30 minutes, and have the basic facts of your project at hand: its title, funder and grant, the people and organisations involved, and the outputs it will produce. A few habits make for a better plan:

- **Start early**, and update the plan at key milestones: it is a living document.
- **Be specific**: say where data is stored and how it is backed up, not only that it is "stored securely".
- **Think beyond the project**: who can reuse the outputs, under which license, and for how long they are kept.
- **Involve your team**, and ask your library, IT, or data steward when you are unsure about storage or preservation.

:::info Administrator setup required
Your organisation must have set up at least one blueprint and one asset template. If no blueprint appears when you create a plan, or no template when you add an asset, contact your OpenCDMP administrator. See [Blueprints and asset templates](/docs/using/blueprints.md) for what they are.
:::

## Step 1: log in

Open your OpenCDMP address, click **Log in**, and sign in with your account or your institution's sign-in. The [Dashboard](/docs/using/home.md) opens. The first time you open a redesigned page, a short [guided tour](/docs/using/home.md#guided-tours) shows what is where; follow it with **Next** or **Skip** it.

## Step 2: start a plan and choose a blueprint

Click **New Plan** under **My Plans** in the side navigation menu. On the **Select Blueprint** step, pick the blueprint that matches your funder or project; selecting one shows a preview of its sections and questions on the right. The default one is usually a good start. If the blueprint asks for your consent, tick the box, then click **Proceed with this blueprint**.

Already have a plan in another tool? Click **Import Plan** instead (see [Import a plan](/docs/using/plans/imports.md)).

![The Select Blueprint step, with the Import Plan card and the blueprint list on the left and the preview of the selected blueprint on the right](/images/plans/create-a-plan/plan-editor-first-step.png)
*Choosing a blueprint*

See [Create or edit a plan](/docs/using/plans/create-or-edit-a-plan.md) for the details, including [Creation Consent](/docs/using/plans/create-or-edit-a-plan.md#plan-creation-consent).

## Step 3: answer the plan questions

The plan editor opens, with the sections of your blueprint in the table of contents on the left and the plan actions in the toolbar at the top. Work through the sections: the plan's title, description, and language, then whatever your blueprint asks, such as contacts, funding, researchers and organisations, licenses, and the plan's **Access**. Funders, grants, people, and organisations are [references](/docs/using/references.md): search for them, or add them with **Insert it manually** when they are not listed.

Questions marked with an asterisk are required, and the **Required fields** counter in the table of contents tracks what is left. Click **Save** whenever you like: saving needs only the title, so you can come back later.

![The plan editor, with the toolbar across the top, the table of contents on the left, and the first section of the plan in the center](/images/plans/create-a-plan/plan-editor.png)
*The plan editor*

See [Fill in a plan](/docs/using/plans/fill-in-a-plan.md) for every kind of question, and [The plan editor](/docs/using/plans/create-or-edit-a-plan.md#the-plan-editor) for the toolbar and saving.

## Step 4: add assets

Assets describe your research outputs, one per output: a dataset, a piece of software, a protocol, and so on. In a section that accepts assets, click **Add new Asset**, choose **Fill in myself** (or import the details from a published record, or copy an existing asset), choose a template, and name the asset. Then answer its questions the same way as the plan's, and save.

See [Create or edit an asset](/docs/using/assets/create-or-edit-an-asset.md) and [Prefill an asset](/docs/using/assets/prefill-an-asset.md).

## Step 5: invite your collaborators

Click the **Collaborators** button in the toolbar, then **Share**. Pick someone you have worked with before, or enter an email address, choose a role (for example **Editor** for a section of the plan, or **Reviewer** to comment), and click **Send invite**. You can still invite people after finalizing, but the roles of collaborators already on the plan can't change then.

See [Work together](/docs/using/plans/invite-collaborators.md) and [Comments and review](/docs/using/comments.md).

## Step 6: check and finalize the plan

When the **Required fields** counter is complete, decide who should see the plan: set its **Access** to **Public** to share it on Public Plans once finalized, or keep it **Restricted Access** (see [Who can see your plan](/docs/using/plans/visibility.md)). If your organisation offers evaluators, **Evaluate** checks the plan against a standard (see [Evaluate a plan or asset](/docs/using/plans/evaluators.md)).

Then save, click the status pill at the start of the toolbar, and choose the finalized status. The **Finalise this item?** dialog lists anything still missing, with **Fix fields** to jump there, and asks which draft assets to finalize with the plan. Draft assets you leave out are removed from the plan. Click **Finalise plan** to confirm.

![The Finalise this item? dialog, with the Plan panel reporting that the plan cannot be finalized, the section that needs input with its Fix fields button, and the draft assets of the plan](/images/plans/plan-workflow/finalize-dialog.png)
*The Finalise this item? dialog*

See [Change status and finalize a plan](/docs/using/plans/plan-workflow.md).

## Step 7: download the plan

Click **Download** in the toolbar, or in the **...** menu of the Plan overview, and choose a format: **PDF** to send to a funder, **Document** to keep editing in Word, or a machine-actionable format. See [Download a plan or asset](/docs/using/download.md#which-format).

## Step 8: publish the plan (optional)

To get a DOI, click **Publish** on the finalized plan, choose a repository such as Zenodo and the account to publish under, review what will be sent, and click **Publish**. See [Publish a plan](/docs/using/plans/deposit-a-plan.md).

## Next steps

- Find your plans again on [My Plans](/docs/using/plans/my-plans.md); a card opens the plan's [overview](/docs/using/plans/plan-overview.md).
- Keep the plan current: start a [new version](/docs/using/plans/versions.md) at each milestone, and compare versions to see what changed.
- Something not working? See [Troubleshooting for users](/docs/using/troubleshooting.md), or open the **Support** menu in the top bar.
